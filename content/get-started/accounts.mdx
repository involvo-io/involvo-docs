# Do I need an account?

No. You can browse everything on INVOLVO without one.

Without an account you can look through the feed and the calendar, search, apply every filter, and open any event to read what it is, when and where it runs, and who is organizing it.

An account matters once you want to do something rather than just look.

## What an account adds

- **Save events and get reminders**, so an interesting event does not depend on you remembering it.
- **Apply or register** for events that collect registrations through INVOLVO.
- **Check in** when you arrive, which also lets you see who else is there.
- **Keep a record** of the events you have taken part in.
- **Connect with people** you met at an event.
- **Follow organizations** whose events you want to hear about.

## Registering without an account

Some organizers turn on guest registration for their event. Where they have, you can register with just your details and no account.

If you later decide you want an account, you can turn that guest registration into a full one and keep the registration you already made. You are not starting over.

Guest registration is the organizer's choice, event by event, so it will not be available everywhere.

## Signing in

You can sign in with an email address and password, or ask for a magic link — a one-time link sent to your email that signs you in without a password.
