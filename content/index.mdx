# INVOLVO Documentation

INVOLVO is a place to find events and opportunities for young people, and to sign up for
them: workshops, conferences, training courses, seminars, hackathons, competitions, youth
exchanges, roundtables, forums, volunteering and informal meetups. Some happen in person,
some are online, and some are held in another country but open to people applying from yours.

These pages explain how to use it — as somebody looking for something to attend, and as an
organization publishing and running events.

## One thing worth knowing early

INVOLVO does not organize the events listed on it. They are run by non-profit organizations,
community and student groups, businesses and independent organizers. INVOLVO lists their
events and, for many of them, handles registration — so questions about the program, the
venue, costs or who gets selected go to the organizer, not to us. See
[What is INVOLVO](/get-started) and [How INVOLVO works](/get-started/how-involvo-works).
