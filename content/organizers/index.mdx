## Publishing

There are [two ways an event gets on INVOLVO](/organizers/publishing/two-ways), and they behave
differently. If you are part of the organization running it, you
[publish it yourself](/organizers/publishing/publish-your-event) and it appears straight away.
If you are not, you [share an event you found](/organizers/publishing/share-an-event) and it
goes to [review](/platform/moderation) first.

## Running it

[Applications and participants](/organizers/running/applications-and-participants) covers the
two stages organizers most often conflate — deciding on applicants, then getting the people you
accepted to confirm. [Check-in day](/organizers/running/check-in-day) is the practical guide to
the door. [Feedback and analytics](/organizers/running/feedback-and-analytics) is what you get
back afterwards.

## Limits

What your organization can do — participants per event, how often you can message followers —
depends on its plan. See [Plans and limits](/organizations/plans-and-limits).
