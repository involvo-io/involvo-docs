# Your account

The settings that decide what you are shown and how INVOLVO reaches you.

<Cards>
  <Cards.Card title="Profile, language and location" href="/participants/account/profile-and-preferences" arrow />
  <Cards.Card title="Notifications" href="/participants/account/notifications" arrow />
</Cards>

Two of these settings do more than they look like they do: your **city** decides which events
the calendar can show you at all, and the **languages you can attend in** decide which online
events reach you.
