# Notifications: email and Telegram

INVOLVO can reach you in three places: inside the app, by email, and on Telegram.

## The three channels

**In the app** — always on. Notifications appear in INVOLVO whatever else you have turned off.

**Email** — on for the things that concern you directly.

**Telegram** — off until you connect your account, then the fastest of the three. Worth connecting if you do not check email often; a notification about an event closing tomorrow is not much use found next week.

## What you can turn on and off

Four categories, each set separately for email and for Telegram:

- **Event updates** — changes to events you are involved with.
- **Social activity** — friend requests and what your friends are doing.
- **Daily digest** — a daily summary of what is new.
- **Weekly digest** — the same idea, once a week.

The two digests are the ones to think about. Daily suits someone actively looking for something to attend; weekly suits everyone else. Turning both on is usually one too many.

**By default, digests are off for email and on for Telegram**, while event updates and social activity are on for both. Nothing here is set in stone — the settings matrix lets you pick any combination.

## What you cannot turn off

Messages needed for your account to work — sign-in links, password resets, and similar — are always sent. They are not marketing and there is no unsubscribe for them, because switching them off would lock you out.

## Connecting Telegram

Connect it from your settings. There is a one-off reward in Impact Stars for doing so, and it becomes your fastest channel. See [Earning and spending Stars](/platform/earning-and-spending-stars).

## Unsubscribing

Every non-essential email has an unsubscribe link, and you can change any of it from your settings at any time. Unsubscribing from email does not affect Telegram or in-app notifications — they are set separately.
