# Platform

The parts of INVOLVO that cut across everything else: what other people can see about you,
what gets checked before an event appears, and the two things called Points and Stars.

## Privacy and your data

[What other people can see about you](/platform/what-others-can-see) is the practical picture:
what an attendee sees, what your friends see, what the organizing team sees, and what an
anonymous visitor sees.

The formal documents live on the INVOLVO wiki, in the
[Legal Documentation](https://wiki.involvo.io/books/legal-documentation) book:

- [Privacy Policy](https://wiki.involvo.io/books/legal-documentation/page/privacy-policy)
- [Terms of Service](https://wiki.involvo.io/books/legal-documentation/page/terms-of-service)

## Safety and moderation

Events added by somebody other than the organizer are reviewed before they appear.
[What moderation checks](/platform/moderation) describes what that review looks at.

## Points and Stars

Two things that sound alike and are not. [Impact Points vs Impact
Stars](/platform/impact-points-and-stars) separates them,
[earning and spending Stars](/platform/earning-and-spending-stars) covers what they are for,
and [referrals and daily rewards](/platform/referrals-and-rewards) covers the other ways to
get them.
