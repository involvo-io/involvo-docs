# Referrals and daily rewards

Two ways to build up Impact Stars and plan time without earning them one event at a time: turning up regularly, and bringing other people.

## Daily rewards

Sign in and claim your reward each day. The run works in cycles of seven days, and the amount grows as the cycle goes on — the seventh day is worth considerably more than the first.

On the seventh day there is also a draw for a code giving you a few free days of a paid plan.

**Missing a day breaks the run**, and you start the cycle again. There is a short grace period after midnight, so claiming late at night and then again the next evening does not punish you — but a skipped day is a skipped day.

The point of the escalation is that it only pays properly if you come back. There is no way to shortcut it.

## Referrals

You have a referral link and a code. Anyone signing up through them is counted as yours.

**A referral only counts once the person actually uses INVOLVO** — specifically, once they have completed their own first seven-day run of daily rewards. Signing up and disappearing does not count. This is deliberate: the reward is for bringing people who stay, not for collecting registrations.

Because of that, referral rewards arrive with a delay of at least a week after someone joins. That is normal, not a fault.

## The referral ladder

Rewards come at three thresholds of people who became active:

- **Three** — a code for a few days of Plus.
- **Six** — a code for a longer stretch of Pro.
- **Ten** — a T-shirt, arranged by emailing INVOLVO rather than by a code.

Codes arrive by email and have an expiry date, so redeem them rather than saving them for later.

## Worth knowing

Both of these reward the same behavior: using INVOLVO, and bringing people who also use it. Neither can be gamed into a real income, and the limits on gifting Stars exist to keep it that way — see [Earning and spending Stars](/platform/earning-and-spending-stars).

The honest way to use referrals is to send your link to people who would have liked INVOLVO anyway.
